Ticket insurance FAQ

World Cup 2026 ticket insurance FAQ

Fans often mix up ticket protection, travel insurance and refund rules. This FAQ separates the three before you commit to flights, hotels or match tickets.

Is ticket insurance the same as travel insurance?

No. Ticket protection usually relates to the match ticket. Travel insurance may relate to flights, hotels, medical costs, delays or interruption depending on the policy wording.

What is usually not covered?

Buyer regret, a cheaper ticket appearing later, a preferred team not playing in your city, or unofficial resale problems are usually planning risks rather than automatic coverage.
