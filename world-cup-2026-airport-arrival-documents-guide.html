Airport arrival

World Cup 2026 airport arrival documents guide

Landing day is where exhausted fans make small mistakes that create big stress. Keep the right document set ready before you walk to immigration, baggage claim and ground transport.

Your first-night setup matters more than your full itinerary when a late arrival goes wrong.

The arrival bundle to keep in your hand

  • Passport and entry approval if required.
  • First hotel address, not just the booking app.
  • Return or onward transport proof.
  • Insurance contact number and policy summary.
  • Match ticket confirmation for the next 48 hours.
  • Phone charger, local SIM plan or eSIM details.

What officers and airline staff often want fast

They usually want simple, believable trip basics: where you are sleeping first, how long you are staying, and how you plan to leave. Fans overcomplicate this by opening ten tabs instead of showing one clean hotel confirmation and one onward booking.

Good first-night strategy for match travelers

If your flight lands after dark or close to a match day, keep one low-drama first-night plan. That usually means an easy transfer hotel, flexible check-in and enough buffer to recover before moving to a stadium district.
