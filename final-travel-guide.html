Final guide

World Cup 2026 final travel guide

The final in New York New Jersey will be one of the highest-demand travel moments of the tournament. Plan hotels, airports and stadium access before prices and availability tighten.

Book the trip around one anchor

The final should be treated as the anchor event. Add sightseeing, fan zones or nearby cities after you understand ticket status, hotel terms and return transport from MetLife Stadium.

Where to stay

Manhattan is best for first-time visitors and sightseeing, Newark can reduce airport transfer time, and Meadowlands-area hotels can simplify match day. The tradeoff is nightlife, restaurants and late-night flexibility.

Airport strategy

  • Newark is often the simplest airport for New Jersey-side hotels.
  • JFK and LaGuardia can work well for New York City stays.
  • Compare total transfer time, not just flight price.

Match-day advice

Leave a large buffer for train transfers, rideshare demand and stadium security. Save offline maps, confirm bag rules and decide your return route before kickoff.

Final weekend planning

Arrive at least one day before the final if your budget allows. Same-day arrival creates too much risk from flight delays, hotel check-in timing and stadium transfer pressure. Fans staying after the final should also consider the morning after: airport lines, traffic and rideshare demand can stay elevated.

What to avoid

  • Non-refundable rooms far from both stadium and airport.
  • Tight return flights after a late celebration or extra-time match.
  • Relying on one transport option without a backup route.
  • Crossing the region multiple times on final day.
