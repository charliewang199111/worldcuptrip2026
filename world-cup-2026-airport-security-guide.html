World Cup 2026 airport security guide

World Cup 2026 travel will put pressure on airports, screening lanes, documents and ground transfers. The safest fan plan uses bigger buffers than a normal weekend trip.

Why airport buffers matter

U.S. aviation planning for the tournament highlights safe and efficient handling of increased traffic. For fans, the practical takeaway is simple: do not plan tight airport-to-stadium transfers.
