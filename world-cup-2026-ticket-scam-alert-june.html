Ticket safety

World Cup 2026 ticket scam alert

As demand rises, fans should treat every urgent ticket offer, social-media seller and off-platform payment request as a risk until it is proven safe through official channels.

Fast conclusion

The safest path is simple: start with FIFA ticket information, use official resale or exchange channels when available, avoid pressure tactics, and never let a cheap seat destroy flights, hotels and border planning.

Red flag table

SignalWhy it is riskySafer move
Seller asks for instant transferHard to reverse after a fake listingPause and verify the official route.

Fan checklist

  • Save all receipts and official confirmation emails.
  • Keep hotel rooms flexible until ticket status is clear.
  • Use a second device to check suspicious links before logging in.
  • Do not share account codes, ticket barcodes or app screenshots.
  • Keep a no-ticket fan zone plan as a backup.
