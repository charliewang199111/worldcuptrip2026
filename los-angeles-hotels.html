Los Angeles hotels

World Cup 2026 Los Angeles hotels

Los Angeles hotel planning is about traffic and trip style. A hotel can look close on a map but still create a long match-day transfer to SoFi Stadium.

Best for stadium focus

Inglewood and nearby airport-area hotels can reduce match-day transfer uncertainty. This can be practical for short trips, but visitors should compare restaurants, safety, airport timing and post-match transport.

Best for sightseeing

Santa Monica, Hollywood, Downtown LA and Westside areas suit different travel styles. The best choice depends on whether the trip is about the match only or a longer California visit.

Traffic warning

  • Leave more time than the distance suggests.
  • Check event-day rideshare pickup zones.
  • Avoid tight airport departures after night matches.
