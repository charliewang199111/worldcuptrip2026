Ticket access

World Cup 2026 ticket transfer problem guide

Ticket transfer trouble is stressful because fans usually discover it close to kickoff. The goal is simple: verify the ticket inside the right account before travel, not outside the stadium.

What to check

  • The ticket appears inside the official ticket account or app.
  • The match, seat and date match your plan.
  • You know whether the ticket can be transferred again.
  • You have payment records and seller communication saved.
  • You avoid urgent private offers near stadium gates.
