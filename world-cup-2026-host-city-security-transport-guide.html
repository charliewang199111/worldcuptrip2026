World Cup 2026 host city security and transport guide

Large host cities will not fail because fans lack passion. They fail for visitors when transport, bag rules, security lines and late-night returns are underestimated.

City planning rules

  • Do not assume a host city works like a normal weekend during match windows.
  • Use official local transport pages close to the match date.
  • Build one route to the stadium and one route away from it.
  • Keep a no-ticket fan zone plan separate from a stadium plan.
