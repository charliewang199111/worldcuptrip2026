World Cup 2026 in Dallas

Dallas has a large stadium, strong domestic travel demand and useful airport, hotel and transport search angles for fans planning match trips.

Matches and stadium

AT&T Stadium is the Dallas-area venue for World Cup 2026. The Dallas page is valuable for domestic travelers because it combines a large stadium, multiple airport choices and a spread-out metro area.

Travel planning angles

  • DFW versus Dallas Love Field airport planning.
  • Where to stay: Arlington, Dallas or Fort Worth.
  • Match-day ride, parking and traffic guidance.
  • Heat planning for June and July visitors.

Where to stay

Arlington is closest to the stadium, while Dallas and Fort Worth can be better for restaurants, nightlife or a longer Texas trip. Fans should compare hotel prices with transport time, especially if matches finish late.

Match-day checklist

  • Compare DFW and Dallas Love Field before booking flights.
  • Plan for heat and sun exposure in June and July.
  • Check parking and rideshare instructions before match day.
