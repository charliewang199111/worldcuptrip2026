World Cup 2026 airport buffer planner

Airport buffers are boring until they save a match day. Use this guide to avoid same-day flights, tight cross-border transfers and late arrivals that make tickets stressful.

Fast conclusion

For international fans, arrive at least one day before a key match when possible. For domestic hops, protect the next morning and avoid building a route where one delay ruins two cities.

Planning steps

  • Do not land on match afternoon unless you can miss the event without ruining the trip.
  • Check airport-to-hotel and hotel-to-stadium separately.
  • Build a backup viewing plan if a flight delay happens.
  • Keep documents and insurance details available offline.
