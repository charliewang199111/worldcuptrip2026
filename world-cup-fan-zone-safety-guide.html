World Cup 2026 fan zone safety guide

Fan zones can be the best part of a no-ticket trip, but crowd exits, heat, phone battery and late transport need a plan before kickoff.

A good fan-zone plan includes the exit, not just the screen.

Safety checklist

  • Pick a meeting point outside the crowd before the match starts.
  • Carry water, power bank, ID and one backup payment method.
  • Check bag rules for official events and nearby venues.
  • Know the second-best transit option before the match ends.
  • Leave early if the crowd feels compressed near exits.
