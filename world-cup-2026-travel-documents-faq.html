FAQ

Does a World Cup ticket work as a visa?

No. A ticket helps explain your trip, but it does not replace passport validity, visa rules or electronic travel authorization.

What is FIFA PASS?

FIFA presents FIFA PASS as a planning and event-support product. It can help fans organize trip information, but it does not override immigration controls.

What should be in my document folder?

Passport scan, visa or entry approval, hotel addresses, onward travel proof, ticket confirmation, insurance details and an emergency contact sheet.

How much passport validity is enough?

Do not guess. Check your nationality against the official entry site for each host country and renew early if you are close to expiry.

Community pain points we keep seeing

  • Fans build an itinerary around two countries, then discover they only checked the first border.
  • People save every document inside one phone app, then lose access when roaming, battery or login problems hit.
  • A resale ticket gets treated like official immigration proof, which it is not.
  • Group trips forget that one weak passport in the group can break the whole route.

Best document setup for a match trip

  1. Create one cloud folder and one offline phone folder.
  2. Print the first hotel, first outbound flight and one insurance sheet.
  3. Save border links for each host country before departure.
  4. Keep passport and payment cards in separate places on match days.
