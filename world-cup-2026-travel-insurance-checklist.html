World Cup 2026 travel insurance checklist

Travel insurance for the World Cup is not one decision. Fans need to separate medical coverage, cancellation, delays, luggage, ticket protection and multi-country entry risk.

Does it cover the USA, Canada and Mexico?Some plans vary by countryCoverage territory wording.

Are delays and missed connections covered?Multi-city routes are fragileFlight disruption evidence.
Is luggage delay covered?Match-day gear and documents matterBag report and receipts.

Fan method

Buy insurance after you understand the trip shape, not after everything is locked. A single-city trip, a cross-border route and a final-week itinerary have different risk profiles.
