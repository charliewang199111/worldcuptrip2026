India fans World Cup 2026 travel guide

For fans traveling from India, the biggest issues are long-haul flight timing, entry documents, forex, eSIM, medical coverage and choosing a route that does not require too many exhausting city jumps.

Suggested route logic

Start with one main region: West Coast, East Coast/final week, or Mexico plus one U.S. city. A three-country trip can work, but only if documents, flight buffers and budget are clear before booking.
