Ticket payments

World Cup 2026 ticket chargeback and refund guide

Ticket payment problems create a second-order travel problem: fans freeze hotels and flights while waiting to know whether a purchase actually succeeded. Keep evidence organized before making more non-refundable bookings.

What to do first

  1. Check your official ticket account, not only your bank app.
  2. Save order numbers, screenshots, timestamps and payment emails.
  3. Contact official ticket support before assuming a chargeback is the best route.
  4. Keep hotels flexible until ticket status is clear.
