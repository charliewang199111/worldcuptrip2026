Match-day security

World Cup 2026 stadium bag policy guide

Final stadium rules can vary by venue and event instruction, but fans can plan now by packing light, arriving early and separating essential items from hotel luggage.

Match-day packing rule

Assume security will be strict and lines will be long. Bring only essentials you can carry comfortably, and check official match instructions before leaving the hotel.

Common mistake

Do not arrive at the stadium with luggage after checking out of a hotel. Use hotel storage, station storage or a different schedule so match-day security does not become your biggest problem.
