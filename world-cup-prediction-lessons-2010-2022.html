World Cup prediction lessons from 2010 to 2022

The last four World Cups show why predictions should be flexible: favorites matter, but form, draw path, injuries and one upset can rewrite travel demand overnight.

Travel lesson

When a popular team goes deeper than expected, fans chase tickets late. That can change hotel demand in knockout cities faster than normal travel pages suggest.
