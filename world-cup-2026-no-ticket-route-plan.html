World Cup 2026 no-ticket route plan

You can still build a good World Cup trip without tickets, but the route should be based on fan zones, city atmosphere, transport and hotel practicality.

No-ticket planning rules

  • Do not book a city only because a famous team might play there.
  • Check official city information for fan events as tournament dates approach.
  • Choose hotels around safe late-night return routes.
  • Keep the budget flexible in case official ticket options appear later.
