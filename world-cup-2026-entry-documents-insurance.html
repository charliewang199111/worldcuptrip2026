World Cup 2026 entry documents and insurance

A match ticket does not replace immigration rules. Fans crossing between the USA, Canada and Mexico should check entry documents and insurance coverage before locking a multi-country route.

Document and insurance checklist

  • Check passport validity for every country on the route.
  • Confirm visa, ESTA, eTA or other entry permissions if required.
  • Make sure travel insurance covers every host country you visit.
  • Save policy numbers, emergency contacts and accommodation addresses offline.
  • Do not assume a ticket guarantees entry into any host country.
