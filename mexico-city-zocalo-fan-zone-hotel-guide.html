Mexico City fan zone and Zocalo hotel guide

Mexico City can be one of the strongest no-ticket World Cup trips, but the best plan connects public viewing, hotel area, altitude, rain and late return routes.

Quick conclusion

Stay near the experience you actually want. Zocalo-area stays can suit public viewing and history, Roma/Condesa can suit restaurants and nightlife, and Polanco can suit upscale comfort.
