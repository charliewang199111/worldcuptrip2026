World Cup 2026 in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is a strong gateway city page because fans will search for SoFi Stadium access, airport choices, hotels and local match-day logistics.

Matches and stadium

SoFi Stadium is the Los Angeles-area venue for World Cup 2026. Because the Los Angeles region is spread out, fans should plan around both the stadium location and the neighborhood where they want to spend non-match days.

Travel planning angles

  • LAX arrival planning and ride times to Inglewood.
  • Where to stay for stadium access, beaches or downtown.
  • Traffic and match-day buffer recommendations.
  • Nearby activities for fans staying multiple days.

Where to stay

Inglewood can simplify stadium access, while Santa Monica, Hollywood and Downtown Los Angeles may be better for sightseeing. The right choice depends on whether the trip is built around one match, several days of tourism or a longer California itinerary.

Match-day checklist

  • Plan for traffic and avoid tight dinner or airport connections after the match.
  • Compare LAX transfers with hotel location before booking.
  • Check parking, rideshare and stadium entry guidance before match day.
