World Cup 2026 ticket account login problem guide

Ticket account login trouble is preventable. Do the login test while calm, on stable Wi-Fi, before you leave for the stadium.

Pre-match login checklist

  • Confirm the correct email account.
  • Reset password early if needed.
  • Check two-factor access while traveling.
  • Open the ticket on stable Wi-Fi.
  • Save support links and receipts.
