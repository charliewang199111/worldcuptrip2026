New York hotels

World Cup 2026 New York hotels

New York New Jersey hotel planning is a balance between sightseeing, airport access, stadium transport and final-weekend demand.

Manhattan

Manhattan works best for first-time visitors, restaurants, sightseeing and transit flexibility. It may cost more, and match-day transfers to MetLife Stadium need a generous buffer.

Newark

Newark can simplify airport access and may reduce travel time for New Jersey-side stays. Compare neighborhood, late-night transport and station access before booking.

Meadowlands area

Hotels near the stadium can reduce match-day friction but may be weaker for non-match-day sightseeing. They can be useful for final-focused trips if prices remain reasonable.

Booking advice

  • Use flexible rates while ticket status is uncertain.
  • Compare airport, stadium and sightseeing time together.
  • Avoid tight flights the morning after the final.
