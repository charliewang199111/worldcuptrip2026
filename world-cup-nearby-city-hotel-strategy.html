World Cup 2026 nearby city hotel strategy

When host-city hotels spike, a nearby city can save money. But the decision only works if the late-night return and airport plan are realistic.

Nearby-city hotels are a total-cost decision, not just a nightly-rate trick.

When it can work

  • You have a car or a reliable rail/bus route.
  • The match is not followed by an early flight.
  • The hotel saves enough to cover transport and time.
  • You are traveling with a group that can split transfer cost.

When it is risky

If the final leg depends on scarce late-night rideshare, a far hotel can become more expensive than staying closer. Families and first-time visitors should be more conservative.
