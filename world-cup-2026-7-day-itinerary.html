One-week route

World Cup 2026 7 day itinerary

Seven days is best for one anchor city plus one nearby add-on. The goal is to see the match, enjoy the city and avoid turning the trip into airport work.

Sample 7 day plan

DayPlanWhy
1Arrive and recoverProtect against long-haul delays.
2City walk and stadium route testReduce match-day stress.
3Match dayKeep the schedule light.
4Fan zone or rest dayUse the city, not airports.
5Second city or nearby tripOnly if transfer is simple.
6Second match or fan eventKeep a backup plan.
7DepartureAvoid early flights after late matches.

Best 7 day route types

Northeast city pairs, Mexico city pairs and one-city final-week plans work better than long cross-country routes. If you only have one week, do not chase too many stadiums.
