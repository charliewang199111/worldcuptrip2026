Ticket protection

World Cup 2026 ticket protection guide

Ticket protection and travel insurance are not the same thing. Fans should understand what protects a match ticket, what protects flights and hotels, and what is still their own planning risk.

Why this matters now

FIFA ticket support material recommends considering ticket protection for unforeseen circumstances. That is useful, but fans still need to compare policy wording, covered reasons and what happens to flights or hotels if the match plan changes.

Protection comparison

ItemWhat may protect itQuestion to ask
Match ticketTicket protection or official ticket termsWhat reasons are covered?
FlightsTravel insurance or airline rulesAre delays or cancellation reasons covered?
HotelsRefundable rate or travel insuranceIs prepaid lodging included?
Resale issueOfficial platform rulesIs the seller official?

What fans should not assume

Do not assume ticket protection covers buyer regret, cheaper seats appearing later, a team not playing where you hoped, or an informal resale problem. Keep travel insurance and ticket protection decisions separate.
