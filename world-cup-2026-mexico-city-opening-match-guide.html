Mexico City opening match travel guide

The Mexico City opener is a high-emotion, high-friction trip. Treat it as a match-first itinerary: arrive early, protect your ticket access, and keep the first night simple.

Recommended plan

Arrive at least one day before the match, stay in an area with a clear route to the stadium, and avoid adding a second city immediately after the opener unless you have a full transfer day.
