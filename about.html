About World Cup Trip 2026

World Cup Trip 2026 is an independent planning website for football fans preparing trips around the 2026 tournament in Canada, Mexico and the United States.

What we do

We publish practical travel planning content: host city guides, match route ideas, hotel area notes, ticket research guidance, time-zone pages, fan tips and tournament explainers.

How we organize content

Our pages are designed around real planning questions: where to stay, how to reach a stadium, which airport makes sense, how to plan a multi-city route and how to avoid common match-day mistakes.

Independence

World Cup Trip 2026 is not affiliated with FIFA, the FIFA World Cup, host cities, stadium operators, teams, ticketing platforms or travel providers. Names of tournaments, cities, stadiums and organizations are used for identification and planning context.

Advertising and affiliate links

The site may display advertising or use affiliate links. If visitors click certain links or make purchases through partners, World Cup Trip 2026 may earn a commission at no additional cost to the visitor. Editorial recommendations should remain useful and clearly separated from paid placement.
