World Cup 2026 family no-ticket day plan

A no-ticket day can work well for families if the plan is comfortable, short enough, and close to food, shade, restrooms and reliable transport.

Best family structure

Use one main public viewing plan, one backup indoor viewing option, and a hotel route that does not depend on late-night rideshare queues. Keep the day shorter than an adult fan-zone plan.
