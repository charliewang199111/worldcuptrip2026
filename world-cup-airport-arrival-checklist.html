Airport arrival

World Cup 2026 airport arrival checklist

The first 90 minutes after landing decide whether the rest of the day feels calm or chaotic. Use this checklist before you chase the cheapest ride.

Fix phone data and hotel routing before leaving the terminal.

First 90 minutes

MinuteActionWhy
0-15Turn on eSIM or roamingMaps and rideshare need data
15-30Confirm hotel address and check-in timeMany mistakes happen at similar hotel names
30-45Compare rail, official taxi, rideshare and shuttleThe best option changes by city and luggage
45-90Message your group and save offline routeArrivals can split groups fast

Do not leave the airport until these are done

  • Your phone has working data outside airport Wi-Fi.
  • You have the hotel address in English and local format when relevant.
  • You know whether your next match ticket is accessible offline or inside an app.
  • You have a backup route if rideshare prices surge.
