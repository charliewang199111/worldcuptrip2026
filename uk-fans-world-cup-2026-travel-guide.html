UK fans World Cup 2026 travel guide

UK fans need a plan that handles long-haul flights, entry documents, late-night match returns, medical insurance and realistic city clusters.

Best planning order for UK fans

  1. Choose one anchor city or England match scenario.
  2. Compare direct flight gateways and onward routes.
  3. Check entry requirements before booking non-refundable plans.
  4. Buy suitable medical and cancellation cover.
  5. Keep at least one recovery day after arrival.
