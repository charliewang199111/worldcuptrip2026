First night

World Cup 2026 airport to hotel first night guide

The first night should be boring. A clean landing, easy hotel transfer and enough sleep are better than a heroic same-day match sprint that risks the whole trip.

First-night hotel decision

Arrival typeBest hotel styleReason
Late international arrivalAirport or simple transfer hotelReduces first-night failure
Next-day matchTransit-access hotelBalances sleep and stadium access
Family tripFlexible check-in hotelLess stress with bags
Cross-border routeDocument-friendly first stopEasy to explain itinerary

First-night checklist

  • Hotel address printed and saved offline.
  • Transfer route selected before landing.
  • Backup ride option if public transport is closed.
  • Match essentials kept in carry-on.
  • Next-day stadium route checked before sleep.
