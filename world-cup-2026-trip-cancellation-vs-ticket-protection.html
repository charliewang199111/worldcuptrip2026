Trip cancellation vs ticket protection for World Cup 2026

Trip cancellation insurance and ticket protection are not interchangeable. Fans need both concepts separated before they spend on flights, hotels and match tickets.

Best fan strategy

Protect the parts with the highest loss first: long-haul flights, final-week hotels and medical exposure. Treat tickets as their own contract with separate terms.
