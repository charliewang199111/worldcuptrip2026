Airport safety

New York airport taxi scam guide for World Cup 2026 fans

New York will be one of the highest-pressure World Cup travel markets because the final is in the New York/New Jersey area. The first risk for many visitors is not the stadium. It is the airport exit.

Fast rule

Do not accept rides from people approaching you inside the terminal, baggage claim or curb area. Use official taxi lines, licensed app pickup zones, public transit, hotel shuttles or pre-booked transfers you can verify.

World Cup fan checklist

  • Save the exact hotel address offline before landing.
  • Keep passport, ticket account, wallet and phone with you, not in stored luggage.
  • Do not hand your suitcase to anyone who is not part of an official service.
  • If a ride price is unclear, walk away and use an official queue.
  • Build the first night around safety and sleep, not a tight sightseeing plan.
