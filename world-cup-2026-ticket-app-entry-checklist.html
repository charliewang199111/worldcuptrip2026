Ticket access

World Cup 2026 ticket app and stadium entry checklist

The most expensive ticket is useless if your phone, login, payment record or bag plan fails at the gate. Use this page as a match-day access check before leaving the hotel.

Before leaving the hotel

  • Open the official ticket account while on stable Wi-Fi.
  • Confirm the match, seat, name details and transfer status.
  • Charge the phone and carry a power bank.
  • Save passport or ID access details separately from the phone.
  • Check stadium bag rules and remove luggage from the route.

Entry risk table

If something goes wrong

Do not buy a replacement ticket from a stranger outside the stadium. Move to official support, keep screenshots or email receipts, and document the issue for any later ticket, payment or insurance conversation.
