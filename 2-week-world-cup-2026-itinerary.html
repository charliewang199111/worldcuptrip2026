Two-week route

2 week World Cup 2026 itinerary for fans

Two weeks is enough for a strong World Cup trip, but only if you resist the temptation to cross the continent every few days.

Recommended two-week structure

DaysPlanTip
1-3Arrival city and first matchStay near transit, not only nightlife.
4-6Second city or same clusterUse one travel day, not match-day flying.
7-9Fan-zone day plus match dayKeep one lighter day for recovery.
10-12Third city or knockout anchorUse flexible hotels if team path matters.
13-14Final buffer and departureAvoid early departures after late matches.

Three sample two-week routes

Northeast: Boston -> New York New Jersey -> Philadelphia is the simplest for lower flight stress. West: Los Angeles -> Bay Area -> Seattle or Vancouver works for scenic pacing. Mexico-first: Mexico City -> Guadalajara -> Monterrey gives culture and opening-week energy before adding one U.S. city.

Common mistake

The biggest two-week mistake is treating every transfer like a normal vacation flight. World Cup travel days include stadium crowds, hotel checkout friction, airport pressure and late-night match fatigue.
