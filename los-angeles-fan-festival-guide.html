Los Angeles FIFA Fan Festival guide

Los Angeles is one of the best no-ticket World Cup trip ideas because the fan festival layer can turn a stadium-miss into a real city trip.

Fast conclusion

If you do not have match tickets, plan LA around official fan programming, transit-aware hotels, beach or city recovery days, and a flexible West Coast route to the Bay Area, Seattle or Vancouver.

Hotel notes

Do not book only by map distance to the stadium. In LA, late-night return route, rideshare pickup pressure and neighborhood comfort often matter more than raw miles.
