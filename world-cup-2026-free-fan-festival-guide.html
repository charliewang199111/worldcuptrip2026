World Cup 2026 free fan festival guide

Fan festivals can be the best World Cup experience for fans without match tickets. The smart plan is not just “show up”: crowds, heat, weather, food lines and return routes matter.

Best fan festival plan

Arrive before peak match time, carry a light kit, pick a meeting point, and know the exit route before the final whistle. For popular teams, treat public viewing like a stadium event.
