World Cup 2026 Mexico entry guide

Mexico host cities can anchor a culture-rich World Cup trip, but entry documents, insurance and onward U.S. transfers should be planned before the match week rush.

Mexico entry checklist

  • Check Mexico entry requirements for your nationality.
  • Keep accommodation and onward travel details available.
  • Plan for heat, altitude and longer transfer days.
  • Confirm insurance covers Mexico and any U.S. or Canada leg.
  • Do not schedule an international transfer too close to kickoff.
