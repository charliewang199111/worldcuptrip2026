Match day

World Cup 2026 match-day transport checklist

The most important transport plan is the return plan. Make it before the match, while your phone battery and attention are still full.

A route is not complete until it includes the way back.

Before leaving the hotel

  • Save the stadium route and return route offline.
  • Charge phone and power bank.
  • Screenshot ticket, hotel address and group meeting point.
  • Check bag rules and weather.
  • Set a backup pickup point away from the main exit crowd.
