Phone data

World Cup 2026 arrival day phone data plan

Your phone is your ticket, map, translator, payment tool and emergency contact. Arrival day is the wrong time to discover that data, roaming or app login is broken.

Before landing

  • Install or prepare eSIM/roaming before departure.
  • Save hotel address and stadium route offline.
  • Log in to ticket and email accounts on Wi-Fi.
  • Carry a power bank and charging cable.
  • Keep passport and reservation details accessible offline.
