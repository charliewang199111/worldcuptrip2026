Insurance for fans

World Cup 2026 travel insurance for fans

Fans do not need insurance for every small inconvenience. They need protection for the expensive problems: emergency medical care, missed connections, long delays and multi-city plans that break.

What fans should check first

  • Emergency medical coverage and medical evacuation language.
  • Trip delay rules, including how many hours must pass before benefits start.
  • Trip interruption reasons and what documents are required.
  • Whether USA, Canada and Mexico are all covered if crossing borders.
  • Whether event tickets are covered, excluded or handled separately.

Practical note

Insurance is not a shortcut around risky ticket choices. Use official ticket guidance, keep receipts, and do not assume a policy will reimburse buyer regret, price changes or informal resale problems.
