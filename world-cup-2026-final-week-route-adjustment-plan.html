Final week

World Cup 2026 final week route adjustment plan

The final week is when many trips need a route reset. The right move is usually to simplify: one main base, one backup option, and one buffer that protects the final or semifinal.

Final week adjustment table

Adjustment rules

  • Do not add extra sightseeing if it breaks your next-match route.
  • Keep refundable hotels until the semifinals are known.
  • Use the same city base if the final or semifinal is your main goal.
  • Move flights earlier, not later, when demand starts climbing.
