World Cup 2026 ticket terms and refund guide

Before a fan builds flights and hotels around a match, they need to understand ticket terms, refund limits, ticket protection and what travel insurance does not solve.

Fan checklist

  • Read official ticket terms before buying.
  • Save confirmation emails and receipts.
  • Keep ticket decisions separate from hotel cancellation decisions.
  • Do not assume buyer regret is covered.
  • Use official ticket and support channels first.

FAQ

What is the World Cup 2026 refund policy?

Check official FIFA ticket terms and support pages. Refund outcomes depend on official rules, ticket product and the reason for the request.

Does travel insurance refund match tickets?

Not automatically. Event tickets may be excluded or handled under separate wording, so read both ticket terms and policy wording.
