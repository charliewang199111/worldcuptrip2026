FIFA PASS

FIFA PASS visa interview guide for World Cup 2026 fans

FIFA PASS is useful for some fans, but it is not a visa and it is not a guarantee of entry. Treat it as supporting evidence inside a complete travel document plan.

The safest message for fans: FIFA PASS can support a trip story, but official immigration rules still control the outcome.

Do not overstate it

The highest-risk misunderstanding is telling yourself that a World Cup product replaces a visa, ESTA, eTA or admission decision. It does not. Your strongest preparation is a clean document set: passport, official entry approval, first hotel, return plan, insurance and ticket confirmation.

Visa interview folder template

  • Passport and appointment confirmation.
  • FIFA PASS or event-related confirmation if applicable.
  • Match ticket confirmation or official ticket account proof.
  • Hotel reservation for the first city.
  • Return or onward flight plan.
  • Employment, study, family or financial documents requested by the official process.
