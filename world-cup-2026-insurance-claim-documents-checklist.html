World Cup 2026 insurance claim documents checklist

Insurance only helps if you can prove what happened. For World Cup travel, that means saving receipts, airline notices, medical records and booking confirmations before the trip becomes chaotic.

Claim document table

ProblemSave thisWhy
Flight delayAirline delay notice and receiptsProves timing and extra cost
Medical visitMedical report and payment recordSupports emergency claim
Lost luggageAirline baggage reportStarts the evidence trail
Hotel cancellationBooking terms and cancellation emailShows what was refundable
Trip interruptionNew transport receiptsDocuments route change cost

Phone folder setup

  1. Create a folder named World Cup Claims.
  2. Save policy PDF, emergency number and booking receipts.
  3. Photograph paper receipts before they fade or get lost.
  4. Keep airline and hotel emails, even if the app shows the same booking.
