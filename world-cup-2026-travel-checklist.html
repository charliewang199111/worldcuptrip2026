Fast rule

If a plan depends on same-day arrival, a non-refundable hotel, an unverified ticket seller and a large bag near a stadium, the risk stack is too high. Fix the weakest link before booking more.

FAQ

What should be on a World Cup 2026 travel checklist?

Tickets, hotels, insurance, documents, airport timing, luggage storage, stadium bag rules, phone data and a fan-zone backup plan.

When should I finish the checklist?

Do the first pass before non-refundable bookings, then review again two weeks before departure.
