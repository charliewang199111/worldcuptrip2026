Trip protection

World Cup 2026 trip protection guide

Fans often search for insurance when what they really want is broader trip protection: fewer non-refundable decisions, more timing buffer and a backup plan for expensive match days.

The strongest protection is a mix of policy coverage and flexible booking choices.

The refund myth

The most dangerous idea is that an insurance policy will unwind every bad booking. In practice, fans are usually safer with refundable hotels, a less aggressive arrival window and a second route option for high-stakes match days.

Three fan profiles

  • Final-week traveler: pay more for flexibility because hotel spikes are brutal.
  • Road-trip group: protect transport timing and border paperwork first.
  • Family trip: medical and disruption coverage matter more than squeezing every dollar from room rates.

Best next steps

  1. List every prepaid cost: flights, hotels, match tickets, internal transport.
  2. Mark which parts are refundable and which are fixed.
  3. Buy protection after you understand your real risk, not before.
  4. Keep one match-day buffer in every city change.
