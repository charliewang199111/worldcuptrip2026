World Cup 2026 Round of 32 travel guide

The 48-team format creates a Round of 32. For fans, that means more knockout matches, more possible cities and a stronger need for flexible travel planning.

Planning rule

Do not build a Round of 32 route from hope alone. Wait for the bracket path, then choose a city cluster where a missed ticket can still become a fan-zone or city-trip day.
