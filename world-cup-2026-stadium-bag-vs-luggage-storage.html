Bag policy

World Cup 2026 stadium bag policy vs luggage storage

Fans should treat stadium bag policy and luggage storage as two different problems. A bag that is fine for travel can still be wrong for match entry.

Fast answer

Bring only a small match-day bag to stadium or festival areas. Store suitcases and large backpacks before you enter the security perimeter, because some host-city guidance says declined bags may not have storage services.
