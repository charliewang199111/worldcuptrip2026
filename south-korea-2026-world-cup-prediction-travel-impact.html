South Korea World Cup 2026 prediction and travel impact

South Korea is a useful prediction topic because a strong run can pull Asia-Pacific fans into West Coast entry routes, flexible hotel searches and late knockout travel planning.

Prediction angle

South Korea's tournament story usually mixes pace, pressing, experienced stars and emotional fan travel. The practical question is not only how far the team can go, but whether supporters should plan a short West Coast trip or keep enough flexibility for a knockout move.
