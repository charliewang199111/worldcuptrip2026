World Cup 2026 West Coast route: Los Angeles, Bay Area, Seattle and Vancouver

The West Coast route is strong for Asia-Pacific arrivals and scenic trips, but it needs flight and border buffers. Do not treat Los Angeles to Vancouver as a casual hop.

Best use case
