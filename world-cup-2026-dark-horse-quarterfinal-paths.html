Dark horse paths

World Cup 2026 dark horse quarterfinal paths

Surprise teams rarely follow a random script. They usually need a favorable route, a clear identity and one or two heavyweight stumbles elsewhere in the bracket. That matters for travel because quarterfinal runs can shift hotel and ticket demand very fast.

Why quarterfinal scenarios matter more than winner talk

Most so-called dark horses do not need to win the whole tournament to move search traffic and fan logistics. A quarterfinal run is already enough to change where supporters spend, sleep and travel.

Teams to watch through this lens

Morocco, Senegal, Croatia, Denmark and Colombia all make sense as case studies because they can create real bracket trouble without entering the tournament as the most obvious title favorites.
