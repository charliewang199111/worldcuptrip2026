Packing

World Cup 2026 packing list

Pack for the full day, not just the match: airport arrival, summer weather, mobile tickets, fan zones, long walks and late returns.

The best items are small, boring and useful at 11 p.m.

Core packing list

  • Passport, visa/entry documents, booking screenshots and insurance details.
  • Unlocked phone, eSIM or roaming plan, charger and power bank.
  • Comfortable shoes, breathable layers, cap and sunscreen.
  • Small stadium-friendly bag if the venue rules allow it.
  • Backup card and a small amount of local cash.

Do not overpack for match day

Large bags slow you down and may be restricted. Build a separate match-day kit that fits venue rules, then leave the rest at the hotel.
