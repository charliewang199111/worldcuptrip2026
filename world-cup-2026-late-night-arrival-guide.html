Arrival safety

World Cup 2026 late-night arrival guide

Late-night arrivals are cheaper sometimes, but the first hour after landing is when fans are tired, carrying bags, and relying on phone data. Plan it before departure.

Before you land

  • Confirm hotel late check-in.
  • Save hotel address offline.
  • Prepare phone data or eSIM before arrival.
  • Use official taxi, transit or pre-planned pickup points.
  • Avoid same-night long transfers to another city.
