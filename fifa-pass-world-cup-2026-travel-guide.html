FIFA PASS

FIFA PASS World Cup 2026 travel guide

FIFA PASS can help some ticket holders with visa appointment priority, but it is not a visa and it does not guarantee entry into the United States, Canada or Mexico.

What fans should understand

FIFA's travel guidance says FIFA PASS is designed to help eligible ticket holders access prioritized visa appointment scheduling where available. Fans still need to meet each country's immigration rules.
