World Cup 2026 airport luggage storage match day guide

The riskiest luggage day is when a fan lands, cannot check in yet, and wants to go straight to a match or fan festival. Solve storage before you solve transport.

Match-day flow

StepQuestionDecision
LandHow long is immigration and baggage?Add buffer before any event plan.
StoreAirport, hotel or city storage?Choose the pickup location first.
MoveCan you reach stadium without luggage?Use a small day bag only.
ReturnWill crowds block pickup?Pick up before late-night rush if possible.

Practical rule

If the flight lands less than six hours before a match, treat the plan as high risk. One baggage delay, immigration queue or storage detour can consume the margin.
