Medical coverage

World Cup 2026 medical insurance guide

Medical coverage is the part of travel insurance fans should take most seriously, especially for long-haul visitors, families, older travelers and anyone crossing between host countries.

Medical questions to ask

  • Does the policy cover emergency care in the host country?
  • Is emergency medical evacuation included?
  • Are pre-existing conditions limited or excluded?
  • How are prescriptions, urgent clinics and hospital payments handled?
  • What emergency phone number should travelers save?

Who should care most?

TravelerWhy it mattersExtra step
FamiliesMore people, more small emergenciesList every traveler clearly.
Older fansHigher health riskCheck exclusions carefully.
