World Cup 2026 road trip checklist

A World Cup road trip can be cheaper and more flexible than flights, but only if parking, hotels, border documents, luggage and match-day transport are planned before the route gets too ambitious.

Road trip checklist

AreaCheckWhy
RouteLimit long driving days before matchesFatigue ruins match days.
HotelsParking cost and cancellation deadlineCheap rooms can become expensive.
BorderDocuments for every travelerCanada/U.S./Mexico rules differ.
StadiumParking or transit planDriving to venue may not be best.
BagsDo not leave valuables in carTicket account and passport risk.

Better route style

Use clusters instead of a continent-wide sprint. West Coast, Texas/Mexico, East Coast or Canada/U.S. pairings usually produce a more realistic plan than chasing every marquee match.
