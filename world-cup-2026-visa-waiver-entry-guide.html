Entry planning

World Cup 2026 visa waiver and entry guide

Some fans may qualify for visa-waiver style entry, but every traveler still needs to check the rules for each host country on the route. A ticket does not replace entry permission.

What to check first

  • Passport validity for the full trip.
  • USA ESTA or visa requirement if entering the United States.
  • Canada eTA or visa requirement if entering Canada.
  • Mexico entry requirement based on nationality and route.
  • Whether your travel insurance covers all countries visited.
