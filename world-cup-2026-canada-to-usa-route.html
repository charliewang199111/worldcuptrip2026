Canada to USA World Cup 2026 route planner

Canada-to-USA routes can be strong because Vancouver and Toronto connect naturally to bigger regional plans. The mistake is treating the border like a normal city transfer.

Drive or fly?

Driving can be useful when the route is genuinely regional and the group can handle post-match fatigue. Flying is usually cleaner when the next city is not near the border or when a match day is less than one full day away.

Canada-to-USA checklist

  • Check entry documents for both Canada and the United States.
  • Confirm rental-car cross-border permission if driving.
  • Add one buffer day before a must-see match after crossing.
  • Keep hotel cancellation windows visible.
  • Save offline copies of hotel address, return flight and match ticket account details.
