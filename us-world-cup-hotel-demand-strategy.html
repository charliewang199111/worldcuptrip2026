US World Cup 2026 hotel demand strategy

Hotel headlines can be confusing: some markets may look softer than expected, while final, opening, host-nation and famous-team matches can still tighten quickly.

Quick conclusion

Do not treat one demand headline as the whole market. Hold a refundable room in your most likely city, then recheck after ticket phases, team paths and flight prices become clearer.
