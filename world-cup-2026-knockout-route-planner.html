Knockout travel

World Cup 2026 knockout route planner

Knockout travel is difficult because the next city depends on results. The safest plan is to protect buffers, keep hotels flexible and avoid routes that require perfect flights.

Knockout planning rules

  • Hold flexible hotel options in likely route cities.
  • Do not book non-refundable flights before the bracket path is clear.
  • Use fan zones as a backup if ticket access changes late.
  • Leave a recovery day before semifinals or the final when possible.
