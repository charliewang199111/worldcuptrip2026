World Cup 2026 hotel check-in on match day

Hotel check-in can quietly break a match day. If your room is not ready, your bags are still with you, and kickoff is close, every transport choice gets worse.

Best rule

Do not build a plan that requires early check-in. Assume your room opens late, confirm bag storage, and leave for the stadium from a known base rather than from an airport curb.
