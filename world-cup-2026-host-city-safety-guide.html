World Cup 2026 host city safety guide

Host city safety starts with ordinary planning: where you stay, how you return after matches, where your group meets, and which official local updates you follow.

Host city safety checklist

  • Choose hotel areas with realistic late-night returns.
  • Save official city, stadium and transport updates.
  • Set a meeting point before entering crowded areas.
  • Use official ticket and event information.
  • Keep emergency contacts and insurance details offline.
