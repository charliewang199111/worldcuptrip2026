World Cup 2026 FIFA PASS document folder checklist

FIFA PASS can support a travel story, but it should sit inside a broader folder of official documents. A ticket or fan product does not replace immigration approval.

Folder structure

FolderSaveWhy
IdentityPassport scan and emergency contactUseful if phone or bag is lost
EntryVisa, ESTA, eTA or country-specific approvalEntry is separate from ticketing
EventFIFA PASS, ticket account, match confirmationExplains purpose of travel
TripHotel address, return flight, insuranceSupports airport and border questions

Paper copy rule

Keep one printed page with your first hotel, return flight and insurance emergency number. Phone-only planning is fragile when roaming, battery or login problems hit on arrival.
