Dallas hotels

World Cup 2026 Dallas hotels

Dallas-area hotel planning is really a three-area choice: Arlington for stadium access, Dallas for city energy, or Fort Worth for a different Texas base.

Arlington

Arlington is closest to AT&T Stadium and can simplify match-day logistics. Check restaurants, late-night options and airport transfer timing.

Dallas

Dallas can be better for restaurants, nightlife and a longer city trip. The tradeoff is stadium transfer time.

Fort Worth

Fort Worth can work for fans who want a different Texas experience, but it requires clear match-day transport planning.

Summer planning

Heat matters in June and July. Avoid relying on long outdoor walks, and plan rides, hydration and shade.
