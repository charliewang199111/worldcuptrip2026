Ticket coverage

Coverage details for international match tickets

This guide explains the practical difference between ticket protection, travel insurance and official ticket terms for international fans planning World Cup 2026 trips.

International fan checklist

  • Keep ticket confirmation and payment receipts.
  • Save all policy documents offline.
  • Check whether event tickets are included or excluded.
  • Do not rely on insurance to fix unofficial resale risk.
  • Keep border and visa documents separate from ticket coverage.
