Cancellation

World Cup 2026 trip cancellation insurance

Trip cancellation coverage is useful only when the reason is covered. World Cup fans should compare policy language before assuming flights, hotels or tickets are automatically protected.

What cancellation coverage may help with

CostPossible issueWhat to check
FlightsIllness or covered emergencyCovered reasons and proof required.
HotelsNon-refundable bookingWhether prepaid lodging is covered.
TicketsEvent-specific rulesTicket exclusions and documentation.
Multi-city routeOne delay breaks the next cityInterruption and missed connection rules.

What it usually does not fix

Do not expect a standard policy to cover buyer regret, a cheaper ticket appearing later, a team not qualifying for your preferred city, or informal resale problems. Those are planning risks, not automatic insurance benefits.
