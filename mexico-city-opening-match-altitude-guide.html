Mexico City World Cup 2026 opening match altitude guide

Mexico City is special because it combines opening-match demand, Estadio Azteca history, altitude, traffic and first-time visitor logistics.

Do not arrive too tight

If the opening match is your anchor event, avoid landing on match day. Give yourself time for airport transfer, altitude adjustment, hotel check-in and route testing.

Checklist

  • Save your hotel address in Spanish and English.
  • Carry water and avoid overloading the first day.
  • Plan Estadio Azteca transport before leaving the hotel.
  • Keep a backup return route and enough phone battery.
