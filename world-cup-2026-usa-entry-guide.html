World Cup 2026 USA entry guide

Most World Cup 2026 matches are in the United States, so USA entry planning is a core step for international fans, even if the trip starts in Canada or Mexico.

USA entry checklist

  • Check whether you need a visa or ESTA-style authorization.
  • Keep match tickets, hotel addresses and return plans available.
  • Arrive early enough for immigration, baggage and ground transfer.
  • Buy medical insurance that works in the United States.
  • Do not fly into a match city on kickoff afternoon.
