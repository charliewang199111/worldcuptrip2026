Flight routes

World Cup 2026 flight route planner

Flights are not just transportation during the World Cup. They decide how tired you are, how risky your match-day plan becomes, and whether your hotel booking still makes sense.

Flight planning rules

  • Do not fly into a city on the same afternoon as a match.
  • Use morning flights only when the previous night is not a late match.
  • Compare nearby airports, but do not ignore ground transfer cost.
  • Protect final-week travel with an extra night where possible.
