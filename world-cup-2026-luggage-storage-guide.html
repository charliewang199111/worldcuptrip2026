Luggage storage

World Cup 2026 luggage storage guide

Many World Cup travel problems start with one simple issue: you arrive before hotel check-in, carry a suitcase, and still want to go to a match or fan festival.

Fast answer

Do not assume stadiums or fan festivals will hold large luggage. Build a storage plan before match day: hotel desk, airport lockers where available, staffed luggage-storage services, or a flexible check-in strategy.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm hotel luggage hold policy in writing.
  • Keep passport, ticket app, medication and battery pack with you.
  • Do not leave official ticket account access in a stored bag.
  • Use a day bag that can pass stadium or festival screening.
  • Plan the pickup route before late-night transport demand rises.
