Miami hotels

World Cup 2026 Miami hotels

Miami hotel planning depends on whether your trip is match-focused, beach-focused or nightlife-focused. Stadium access can take longer than visitors expect.

Miami Beach

Best for a vacation-style trip with beaches and nightlife. The tradeoff is longer stadium transfer planning, especially after matches.

Brickell and Downtown

Good for restaurants, nightlife and a more urban base. Compare airport transfers and match-day rideshare demand.

Stadium-side stays

Hotels closer to Hard Rock Stadium can reduce match-day distance but may be less useful for beach or city activities.

Heat and weather

Summer heat, humidity and storms can affect transfers. Plan hydration, shade and extra time for outdoor waits.
