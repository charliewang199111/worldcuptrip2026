Fan zone safety

World Cup 2026 fan zone safety checklist

Fan zones can be the best part of a no-ticket trip, but safety planning should happen before the crowd, late-night transport and phone battery problems arrive.

Before you go

  • Choose an official or well-known viewing area when possible.
  • Save the hotel address, meeting point and return route offline.
  • Charge phones and carry a small battery if allowed.
  • Agree on a meeting point if the group gets separated.
  • Keep ID, payment card and emergency contacts secure.
