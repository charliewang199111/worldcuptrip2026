Border timing

World Cup 2026 border wait times guide

Border wait times can turn a smart route into a missed match if fans plan too tightly. Build border buffers the same way you build airport buffers.

Border timing rules

  • Do not cross an international border on the same afternoon as a match.
  • Check official wait-time resources before leaving.
  • Keep documents and accommodation proof easy to access.
  • Use one full transfer day for cross-border road routes.
  • Have a backup plan if the wait is longer than expected.
