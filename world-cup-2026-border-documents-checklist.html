Border checklist

World Cup 2026 border documents checklist

World Cup 2026 is a three-country tournament, but your travel documents are still checked country by country. A match ticket does not replace passport, visa or entry requirements.

Documents to prepare

  • Valid passport for every traveler.
  • Visa, ESTA, eTA or other entry permission if required.
  • Match ticket confirmation and accommodation addresses.
  • Travel insurance policy number and emergency contact.
  • Flight, bus or driving route details for border questions.
